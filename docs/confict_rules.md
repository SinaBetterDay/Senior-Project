# Conflict of Interest Rules

| **Schedule Type** | **Interest** | **Conflict Rule, Threshold, & FPPC Code Section** |
|---|---|---|
| A | Business entity investment | Covers investments of $2,000+. Apply business entity materiality under §18702.1. |
| A-2 | >10% business interest and business position | Business position itself is an economic interest. Apply §18702.1. |
| B | Real-property interest | $2,000+. Materiality depends on the type of governmental decision under §18702.2. |
| C | Source of income | $500+ from source within prior 12 months. Apply §18702.3. |
| D | Source of gifts | $630+ from source within prior 12 months for 2025–2026. Apply §18702.4. |
| E | Travel payment classified as gift or income | If travel payment is a gift, apply §18702.4. If travel payment is income, apply §18702.3. |
